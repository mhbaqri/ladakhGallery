Traditional Foods



Thukpa is a traditional, comforting hot noodle soup that serves as a staple food in Ladakh
Paba is a traditional, highly nutritious staple food of Ladakh made from a dense dough or boiled bread of roasted barley, wheat, buckwheat, and local black peas.

Skyu is a traditional, hearty, soup-based comfort food from Ladakh made with hand-shaped wheat dough pieces cooked in a rich vegetable or meat stew.
Chutagi is a traditional, comforting Ladakhi pasta dish made with hand-shaped bow-tie or pocket-like wheat pasta cooked in a rich, flavorful vegetable or meat

Gyuma is a traditional Ladakhi and Tibetan sausage made from minced mutton or yak meat, rice or roasted barley, and warming spices stuffed into natural animal casings.
Gur-gur Chai is Ladakh's traditional, savory butter tea made by churning strong black tea with yak (or cow) butter, milk, and salt.
