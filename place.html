Beautiful Places of Ladakh

Explore the landscapes and heritage of the region



Leh

Leh is a combination of dramatic Himalayan scenery, monasteries, adventure, and unique high-altitude culture, making it the natural starting point for exploring Ladakh.

Khardung La

Khardung La is one of Ladakh's iconic mountain-pass experiences and a popular stop on the journey from Leh to Nubra Valley.



Suru Valley

Suru Valley is a beautiful valley in the Kargil region of Ladakh, famous for its green landscapes, flowing rivers, and spectacular snow-capped mountains.

Drass

Drass is a scenic gateway between Kashmir and Ladakh, offering spectacular mountain views and a peaceful valley setting



Mulbekh

Mulbekh is a picturesque village in the Kargil region of Ladakh, located on the road between Kargil and Leh. It is known for its dramatic mountains and ancient Buddhist heritage.

Rangdum Valley

Rangdum Valley is a remote and spectacular valley in the Zanskar region of Ladakh, surrounded by rugged mountains and vast high-altitude landscapes



Lamayuru

Lamayuru is one of Ladakh's most distinctive destinations, combining surreal “Moonland” scenery with ancient Buddhist culture.

Turtuk

Turtuk is a peaceful, green oasis in the high-altitude desert of Ladakh, offering a different cultural and natural experience from Leh and the surrounding barren landscapes.



Magnetic Hill

Magnetic Hill is a popular tourist attraction located on the Leh–Kargil highway, about 30 km from Leh. It is famous for an optical illusion that makes vehicles appear to roll uphill on their own when left in a particular position.

Hanle

Hanle is a remote high-altitude village in southeastern Ladakh, famous for its clear skies, vast landscapes, and spectacular views of the night sky.

Pensi La

Pensi La is a spectacular high-altitude pass where the landscapes of Suru and Zanskar meet, making it a memorable stop on the journey toward Zanskar

Zanskar Valley

Zanskar is one of Ladakh's most remote and dramatic destinations, offering pristine Himalayan scenery, ancient monasteries and an unforgettable sense of wilderness
